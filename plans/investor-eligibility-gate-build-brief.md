**Build brief v2** · 29 July 2026 · supersedes v1

Promoting entity: RM Incorporations Ltd, trading as BrightCap (Co. No. 16715037) — *see Open Item 3*
Exemption relied on: FPO 2005 art 48 (high net worth individual) and art 50A (self-certified sophisticated investor)

---

## 0. Assumption this brief rests on

This brief assumes **the shareholders' agreement contains no obligation on the investor to provide further funding, meet capital calls, fund overruns, or give a guarantee or indemnity.**

FPO art 48(8) closes with a proviso applying to every category of qualifying investment: the exemption is available only where the investment is one *"under the terms of which the investor cannot incur a liability or obligation to pay or contribute more than he commits by way of investment."* Art 50A carries the same restriction.

If the SHA does contain such an obligation, arts 48 and 50A are both unavailable and this build does not fix it. Confirm before the release mechanism (§5) goes live. The form and audit trail (§4, §6, §7) are needed on any route and can be built in parallel.

## 3. Correction to the v1 brief: no criterion dropdown

v1 specified *"Which criterion do you meet (dropdown, options depend on category)"* and *"How do you meet it (free text, required)"*. Both deviate from the prescribed form and should be dropped.

The prescribed form is a **No/Yes pair on every condition** — not a selection of one. And the follow-up is a **specific short field per condition**, not a free-text explanation:

| Category | Structure | Detail captured |
|---|---|---|
| HNW | A and B each require an explicit No/Yes | Numeric only: income to nearest £10k, net assets to nearest £100k |
| SCSI | A, B, C, D each require an explicit No/Yes | Org name (A) · company name **and** Companies House number (B) · a **count** (C) · network name (D) |

Do not add a free-text "how do you meet it" box. It gives no better evidence than the prescribed answer, creates a data-minimisation problem, and a free-text field adjacent to the statutory declaration invites argument about whether the statement's meaning was altered. If extra diligence is wanted, keep it as a clearly separate internal note outside the statement and not a condition of the declaration.

The only place a dropdown is genuinely useful is Companies House number formatting for non-UK companies — offer a jurisdiction select alongside a free-text registration number.

## 5. Revised flow

### 5.1 Public page — no gate

Credentials and strategy only. No deal specifics, no numbers, no target returns, no return testimonials. See Open Item 2 — the live site currently breaches this.

CTA to the eligibility form. Keep the CTA neutral in tone; the page copy needs solicitor sign-off, not just the form.

### 5.2 Eligibility form

Steps 1–5 above. Server-side validation only; never trust the client.

**Rejection conditions** — treat the submission as invalid and serve nothing:

1. "None of these apply to me" selected (HNW condition C, SCSI condition E)
2. All substantive conditions answered No
3. Any condition answered Yes with its detail field blank — art 48(2)(b) requires that *completion of the statement indicates* the conditions are satisfied, so an incomplete statement doesn't qualify
4. Contradiction — e.g. Yes to condition A **and** Yes to "none of these apply"
5. Any condition left unanswered

Record rejections too. A record showing you refused someone is worth having.

### 5.3 On valid submission

1. Write the statement record (immutable — §6.2), including an HTML snapshot of exactly what was rendered
2. Compute `expires_at`
3. Upsert the contact, add `investor` to `contact_type`
4. Issue an access token
5. Send a **transactional** email: confirmation + access link

> **Keep the delivery email free of deal content.** If the email describes the opportunity it is itself a financial promotion and needs the full art 48(5)/(5A)/(7) block. Keep the promotion confined to the Summary and the gated page, and the email becomes a simple access notification. Include the warning block anyway if in doubt — it costs nothing.

### 5.4 Not eligible

Neutral message, no deal content, no "come back when you qualify" nudge. Offer a contact route for genuine queries. Do not issue a token.

### 5.5 Access

Token link → server validates certification **at that moment** → renders the gated page and serves the watermarked PDF.

### 5.6 Every subsequent communication

The 12-month test bites at **every** communication, not once at signup. Deal updates, newsletters, follow-ups — all of it must pass the same check at send time. This is the requirement v1 missed entirely, and the realistic failure mode: a warm contact who certified 14 months ago receiving a deal update.

**Operational note for the sales team:** arts 48(1)(a) and 50A cover non-real time and *solicited* real time communications only. A discovery call the investor books themselves is solicited and covered. A call BrightCap initiates is unsolicited real time and outside the exemption.

## 6. Delivery: final recommendation

**Email a tokenised link to a gated page. Do not attach the PDF. Allow a watermarked download from the gated page.**

### Why not email-only

No revocation, no version control, no expiry, no record of who opened it. If the warning block turns out to need correction, you cannot withdraw copies already sent.

### Why not the v1 "email lookup gate"

The v1 technical plan proposed storing the Summary on a page with a contact-lookup email gate — type your email to reveal. Reject this. It is enumerable: anyone who knows or guesses a certified contact's address gets in, and it lets outsiders probe your investor list. It also logs typed strings rather than authenticated people, which is exactly the wrong outcome when the point is a defensible audit trail.

### Recommended mechanism

1. Token: ≥128 bits from a CSRNG, stored **hashed**, never logged in plaintext
2. Bound to `contact_id` + `statement_id` + `document_id`
3. Expiry 14 days; re-issue is self-service and generates a fresh token
4. On redemption: server re-checks certification validity, then streams from a **private** Supabase Storage bucket via a short-TTL signed URL
5. Per-recipient watermark: name, email, timestamp
6. `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` on the handler; never linked publicly. A private bucket means there is no guessable URL to begin with, which is stronger than noindex alone
7. Log every view and every download as a `promotion_communications` row

### On allowing download

Commercially, telling someone considering £420k that they cannot keep a copy costs deals — they will want to show an accountant or a spouse. Allow the watermarked download and log it.

The forwarding risk is real but narrower than it looks: if the recipient forwards it, they are not communicating in the course of business, and it is not a communication BrightCap made. The exposure is loss of control and reputation rather than a direct s21 breach by you. Watermarking is the proportionate control.

## 7. Database schema

Postgres / Supabase. Design principles: certification state is **never** stored as a mutable flag on `contacts`; statements are append-only; financial figures are isolated; and the database itself refuses to record an out-of-window communication.

This deliberately avoids the failure mode flagged in the earlier schema review — compliance state scattered across multiple tables with no single source of truth.

### 7.1 contacts (extend existing)

```sql
alter table contacts
  add column if not exists contact_type text[] not null default '{}',
  add column if not exists marketing_opt_in boolean not null default false,
  add column if not exists marketing_opt_in_at timestamptz,
  add column if not exists privacy_notice_version text;

alter table contacts
  add constraint contacts_type_valid check (
    contact_type <@ array['landlord','investor','vendor','provider','other']::text[]
  );

create index if not exists contacts_type_idx on contacts using gin (contact_type);
create unique index if not exists contacts_email_uidx on contacts (lower(email));
```

`contact_type` is an array, not a scalar. The same person can be a landlord on MarketOffer and an investor on BrightCap, and forcing a single value will cause a data problem later.

### 7.2 investor_statements — append-only

```sql
create type statement_kind as enum ('hnw','scsi');

create table investor_statements (
  id                  uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  contact_id          uuid not null references contacts(id) on delete restrict,
  statement_kind      statement_kind not null,
  instrument          text not null default 'FPO',
  statement_version   text not null,          -- e.g. 'FPO_SCH5_PT1_SI2024-301'
  signed_at           timestamptz not null,
  expires_at          timestamptz not null
                        generated always as (signed_at + interval '12 months') stored,
  signature_typed     text not null,
  declared_full_name  text not null,
  answers             jsonb not null,         -- full No/Yes + detail payload
  qualifying_criteria text[] not null,        -- e.g. '{A}' or '{B,C}'
  declarations        jsonb not null,         -- each tick + its own timestamp
  statement_snapshot  text not null,          -- rendered HTML incl. bold
  ip_address          inet,
  user_agent          text,
  revoked_at          timestamptz,
  revoked_reason      text,
  created_at          timestamptz not null default now(),

  constraint criteria_not_empty check (cardinality(qualifying_criteria) > 0),
  constraint criteria_valid_for_kind check (
    case statement_kind
      when 'hnw'  then qualifying_criteria <@ array['A','B']::text[]
      when 'scsi' then qualifying_criteria <@ array['A','B','C','D']::text[]
    end
  ),
  constraint signed_not_future check (signed_at <= now())
);

create index investor_statements_contact_idx
  on investor_statements (contact_id, signed_at desc);
```

**`statement_snapshot` is the most important evidential column here** and the one most likely to be omitted. If the prescribed wording changes, or a style refactor breaks the bold, you need to be able to show precisely what each investor was shown. Store the rendered HTML.

**Immutability trigger** — only revocation may be written after insert:

```sql
create or replace function investor_statements_immutable()
returns trigger language plpgsql as $$
begin
  if to_jsonb(new) - 'revoked_at' - 'revoked_reason'
     is distinct from to_jsonb(old) - 'revoked_at' - 'revoked_reason' then
    raise exception 'investor_statements rows are immutable except revocation';
  end if;
  return new;
end $$;

create trigger investor_statements_no_update
  before update on investor_statements
  for each row execute function investor_statements_immutable();

create rule investor_statements_no_delete as
  on delete to investor_statements do instead nothing;
```

Re-certification creates a **new row**. Never update in place — the old declaration must survive to evidence promotions made while it was live.

### 7.3 investor_statement_financials — isolated

```sql
create table investor_statement_financials (
  statement_id     uuid primary key references investor_statements(id) on delete restrict,
  income_band      integer,   -- nearest £10,000
  net_assets_band  integer,   -- nearest £100,000
  created_at       timestamptz not null default now(),

  constraint income_rounded  check (income_band is null or income_band % 10000 = 0),
  constraint assets_rounded  check (net_assets_band is null or net_assets_band % 100000 = 0),
  constraint at_least_one    check (coalesce(income_band, net_assets_band) is not null)
);
```

Separate table so the admin view can show *who is certified and when* without exposing net worth. The CHECK constraints enforce the statutory rounding at the database level — you should be unable to store an exact income figure even by accident.

### 7.4 access_tokens

```sql
create table access_tokens (
  id            uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  contact_id    uuid not null references contacts(id) on delete restrict,
  statement_id  uuid not null references investor_statements(id) on delete restrict,
  document_id   uuid not null references documents(id) on delete restrict,
  token_hash    text not null unique,
  expires_at    timestamptz not null,
  first_used_at timestamptz,
  use_count     integer not null default 0,
  revoked_at    timestamptz,
  created_at    timestamptz not null default now()
);
```

### 7.5 documents

```sql
create table documents (
  id                     uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  slug                   text not null unique,
  title                  text not null,
  version                text not null,
  storage_path           text not null,
  promoter_entity_name   text not null,
  promoter_company_number text not null,
  warning_block_version  text not null,
  is_active              boolean not null default true,
  created_at             timestamptz not null default now()
);
```

Promoter details live here, not in hardcoded copy. If a future Summary is issued by the SPV rather than RM Incorporations Ltd, the art 48(5A) block changes per document — see Open Item 3.

### 7.6 promotion_communications — the audit trail

```sql
create table promotion_communications (
  id                  uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  contact_id          uuid not null references contacts(id) on delete restrict,
  statement_id        uuid not null references investor_statements(id) on delete restrict,
  document_id         uuid references documents(id) on delete restrict,
  channel             text not null,   -- 'email' | 'page_view' | 'download'
  exemption_relied_on text not null,   -- 'FPO_ART_48' | 'FPO_ART_50A' | 'FPO_ART_19'
  sent_at             timestamptz not null default now(),

  -- copied from the statement at insert, so a CHECK can enforce the window
  statement_signed_at timestamptz not null,
  statement_expires_at timestamptz not null,

  token_id            uuid references access_tokens(id),
  ip_address          inet,
  user_agent          text,

  constraint within_validity_window check (
    sent_at >= statement_signed_at and sent_at < statement_expires_at
  )
);
```

This is the load-bearing control. Because `statement_id` is **NOT NULL** and the validity dates are copied onto the row, the `within_validity_window` CHECK makes it **structurally impossible to log a communication outside the 12-month window** — no trigger required. Pair it with the rule that nothing sends without logging first, and the database becomes the enforcement point rather than application code that someone can forget to call.

## 8. DB-backed validation

### 8.1 Current certification view

```sql
create or replace view v_contact_certification as
select
  c.id as contact_id,
  s.id as statement_id,
  s.statement_kind,
  s.signed_at,
  s.expires_at,
  (s.id is not null) as is_certified,
  greatest(0, date_part('day', s.expires_at - now())::int) as days_remaining,
  (s.expires_at - interval '1 month') <= now() as due_for_recertification
from contacts c
left join lateral (
  select * from investor_statements st
  where st.contact_id = c.id
    and st.revoked_at is null
    and now() >= st.signed_at
    and now() <  st.expires_at
  order by st.signed_at desc
  limit 1
) s on true;
```

### 8.2 Gate function — call at every send

```sql
create or replace function fn_can_promote(p_contact_id uuid, p_at timestamptz default now())
returns table (allowed boolean, statement_id uuid, reason text)
language sql stable as $$
  select
    st.id is not null,
    st.id,
    case
      when st.id is not null then 'ok'
      when exists (select 1 from investor_statements x
                   where x.contact_id = p_contact_id and x.revoked_at is not null)
        then 'statement_revoked'
      when exists (select 1 from investor_statements x
                   where x.contact_id = p_contact_id)
        then 'statement_expired'
      else 'no_statement'
    end
  from (select 1) _
  left join investor_statements st
    on st.contact_id = p_contact_id
   and st.revoked_at is null
   and p_at >= st.signed_at
   and p_at <  st.expires_at
  order by st.signed_at desc
  limit 1;
$$;
```

Call this at send time. **Never cache the result** and never store a derived boolean on `contacts` — a cached flag is wrong the moment a statement lapses, and a nightly cron that fails silently gives you a stale cache that says everyone is certified.

### 8.3 The boundary day

Art 48(2)(a) refers to *"the period of twelve months ending with the day on which the communication is made."* On the conventional reading, a statement signed on 29 July 2026 is one day outside the 12-month period ending 29 July 2027. The schema above uses `sent_at < expires_at` with `expires_at = signed_at + 12 months`, which is the conservative reading.

Operationally, don't run to the wire: prompt re-certification at **11 months** (`due_for_recertification` above). Worth a one-line confirmation from the solicitor, but it should never matter in practice if the 11-month prompt works.

### 8.4 RLS

```sql
alter table investor_statements enable row level security;
alter table investor_statement_financials enable row level security;
alter table promotion_communications enable row level security;
alter table access_tokens enable row level security;
```

No policies for `anon` or `authenticated` on any of these. All writes go through an Edge Function using the service role; all admin reads go through a server-side route. **The anon key must never touch these tables.** Restrict `investor_statement_financials` to a narrower internal role than the other tables.

### 8.5 Admin view

Read-only, server-rendered. Columns: name, email, statement kind, qualifying criteria, signed date, expiry, days remaining, re-certification due, count of communications, last access. Financial figures behind a separate permission and a logged reveal action — log reads, not just writes.

## 9. GDPR summary

Income and net-asset figures are **ordinary personal data**, not special category under Art 9. No special condition needed.

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| Lawful basis | Legitimate interests (Art 6(1)(f)) with a documented LIA — **not consent** |
| Why not consent | Withdrawal would oblige you to delete the record evidencing your exemption |
| Form control | Privacy notice **acknowledgement**, not a consent tick. Marketing consent separate and optional |
| Minimisation | Only the banded figures the statute asks for. No exact income, no payslips, no bank statements, no free-text explanation |
| Retention | Statement + audit log for 6 years from the last promotion made in reliance on it, then delete. Document the rationale |
| Erasure | Resist under Art 17(3)(e) where retention is necessary for legal claims. Draft the position now; it does not extend to attached marketing data |
| Processors | Art 28 terms with Supabase and Resend; UK IDTA/Addendum where relevant; both in the ROPA and privacy notice |
| Hosting | UK or EU region |
| DPIA | Probably not mandatory. Do a one-page screening and record the conclusion |
| Controller | Must be the same entity named in the art 48(5A) block — see Open Item 3 |

Breach severity is the reason for §7.3: an investor list joined to net-worth figures is a high-harm disclosure, and isolating those two columns is the cheapest mitigation available.
